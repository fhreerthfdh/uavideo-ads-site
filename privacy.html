隐私政策
Privacy Policy

生效及最后更新：2026 年 11 月 7 日 · Effective: November 7, 2026

1. 适用范围与联系

本政策适用于 UAVideo Ads 本地广告管理工具及本公开介绍网站。工具用于运营者自有或获得明确管理授权的 Google Ads 账户。开发者及隐私联系邮箱：honormagic8@gmail.com。

This policy covers the UAVideo Ads local operations tool and its public informational website. The tool serves the operator’s own or explicitly authorized Google Ads accounts. Contact: honormagic8@gmail.com.

2. 访问和处理的数据

在账户持有人授权后，工具可处理 Google Ads 账户标识及配置、广告系列和广告组、素材与广告文案、预算及出价配置，以及展示、点击、费用、转化和转化价值等汇总表现数据。工具还可保存操作者提供的素材、操作指令、API 响应、错误信息及操作回执。

OAuth 或运营者配置的服务账号访问凭据用于调用获授权的 API。工具不要求用户向开发者提供 Google 密码，公开网站不收集登录凭据。

Authorized data may include account identifiers and settings, campaigns, ad groups, creatives, ad copy, budgets, bidding settings, and aggregate performance metrics. Local records may include supplied assets, instructions, API responses, errors, and operation receipts. OAuth or operator-configured service-account credentials enable authorized API calls; Google passwords are not requested.

3. 数据用途

数据用于展示和分析广告表现、制作本地报表、管理素材、执行操作者明确指定的广告操作，以及排查故障、避免重复操作和保留审计记录。仅请求实现这些功能所需的访问权限。

Data is used for reporting, advertising analysis, creative management, explicitly requested account operations, troubleshooting, duplicate-operation prevention, and audit records.

4. 安全、存储及保留 / Security, storage and retention

本地工具通过 HTTPS 向 Google OAuth 和 Google Ads API 发送请求，在传输中使用 TLS 保护数据。凭据由运营者在本机单独配置，不嵌入公开网页或公开源代码；报表和操作回执保存在运营者控制的本地目录中。本公开网站不提供账户数据下载或账户操作接口。

本地数据的访问保护依赖运营者的操作系统账号、文件权限和设备安全设置。应用本身不提供额外的静态文件加密或自动定时删除；本政策不宣称所有本地文件均已加密。运营者应限制设备及备份访问，不公开分享凭据或未脱敏的报表。

OAuth 或服务账号凭据保留至被替换、撤销或删除。报表、API 响应和操作回执按运营、故障排查及审计需要保留，并在不再需要或收到经核实的删除请求后清理；没有统一的自动到期时间。清理范围应包含导出文件及运营者控制的备份。Google Ads 中已创建的资源不会随本地记录删除而自动删除。

Requests to Google OAuth and Google Ads API endpoints use HTTPS/TLS. Credentials are configured separately on the operator’s device and are not embedded in public pages or public source code. Reports and operation receipts are stored in operator-controlled local directories. The public website has no account-data download or account-operation interface.

Local protection relies on operating-system accounts, file permissions and device security. The application does not add encryption at rest or scheduled automatic deletion, and this policy does not claim that all local files are encrypted. Operators must restrict device and backup access and must not publicly share credentials or unsanitized reports.

OAuth or service-account credentials remain until replaced, revoked or deleted. Reports, API responses and receipts are retained for operational, troubleshooting and audit needs, then removed when no longer needed or after a verified deletion request. There is no universal automatic expiry. Deletion includes exports and operator-controlled backups. Removing local records does not remove resources already created in Google Ads.

5. 数据分享与第三方服务

执行账户操作时，相关请求和素材会发送给 Google。操作者可能选择辅助分析、文案或素材处理服务；提供给这些服务的信息应限于完成明确任务所必需的内容，并由操作者按相应授权和服务条款管理。Google OAuth 凭据不应作为提示词或公开内容发送给这些服务。

UAVideo Ads 不出售 Google 用户数据，不将其提供给数据经纪商或用于与该工具功能无关的广告定向。通过 Google API 获得的数据不会被用于训练通用人工智能或机器学习模型。依法必须提供的信息，按适用法律处理。

UAVideo Ads 对从 Google API 收到的信息的使用及向其他应用的转移，将遵守 Google API Services User Data Policy，包括其 Limited Use 要求。

Google receives requests and assets needed for authorized operations. The operator may explicitly select analysis or creative-processing services and must limit shared information to the requested task. OAuth credentials must not be shared as prompts or public content. Google user data is not sold, provided to data brokers, used for unrelated ad targeting, or used to train general-purpose AI or machine-learning models. UAVideo Ads’ use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including Limited Use requirements.

6. 撤销授权与删除 / Your choices and deletion

对于用户 OAuth 授权，可在 Google 账户第三方连接页面移除本应用。对于服务账号，由管理员撤销其 Google Ads 账户访问或停用相关密钥。撤销授权阻止后续使用该授权访问数据，但不会自动清除既有文件。

运营者可停止工具，删除本地凭据、报表、导出文件、API 回执及控制范围内的备份。获授权使用者也可发邮件至 honormagic8@gmail.com，注明“UAVideo Ads 数据请求”，说明希望访问、更正、导出或删除的数据范围。开发者核实请求者对相关账户的权限后处理，并说明完成情况或依法必须保留的部分。请勿发送密码、令牌或完整密钥。Google 自己保存的数据和广告资源受 Google 的设置、政策及保留要求约束。

For user OAuth, remove this app in your Google Account’s third-party connections settings. For service accounts, an administrator can revoke Ads account access or disable the relevant keys. Revocation blocks subsequent access under that authorization; it does not erase existing files.

Operators can stop the tool and delete credentials, reports, exports, receipts and controlled backups. Authorized users may email honormagic8@gmail.com with the subject “UAVideo Ads data request” and specify the data to access, correct, export or delete. The developer verifies account authority before processing and explains completion or any legally required retention. Do not send passwords, tokens or private keys. Data retained by Google is governed by Google’s controls, policies and retention requirements.

7. 网站访问与政策更新

本介绍网站不设置登录表单，不主动添加分析追踪或广告脚本。网站由 GitHub Pages 托管；GitHub 会按其 隐私声明处理访问日志等数据。政策变更会在本页更新日期并公布。

This informational site has no login form, added analytics trackers, or advertising scripts. GitHub Pages hosts the site and processes access data under GitHub’s privacy statement. Policy updates will be published here with a revised date.

8. Google 数据范围与人工处理 / Google data and human access

本应用的 Google 数据访问范围为获授权的 Google Ads 账户及上述广告数据，不因获得 Google Ads 权限而读取 Gmail 邮件、通讯录或 Google Drive 私人文件。工具不请求最终广告受众的个人身份档案；报表主要处理账户级和广告级的汇总指标。

运营者及获其授权的人员可查看本地报表进行广告分析、支持和故障排查。若运营者将部分报告或素材提供给 OpenAI / Codex 等辅助处理服务，应仅限于明确请求的功能、已获授权且必要的数据，并适用相应服务的数据处理设置与条款；不能据此授权服务商销售数据或训练通用模型。凭据不作为提示词提供。GitHub 仅托管公开的应用介绍和政策页面，并按其政策处理访问日志。

Google access is limited to authorized Google Ads accounts and the advertising data described above. Google Ads authorization does not grant this app access to Gmail messages, contacts or private Google Drive files. The tool does not request individual ad viewers’ identity profiles; reports mainly process account-level and ad-level aggregate metrics.

The operator and explicitly authorized personnel may inspect local reports for analysis, support and troubleshooting. If the operator provides selected reports or assets to assistance services such as OpenAI / Codex, sharing must be limited to the explicitly requested functionality and necessary authorized data under the relevant processing terms and settings. This does not permit sale of the data or general-purpose model training. Credentials are not provided as prompts. GitHub hosts only the public informational and policy pages and processes site access logs under its policy.

本政策实质更新时，会在本页面公布更新日期；若改变既有授权数据的使用方式，将在该变更生效前通知受影响的授权使用者，并在需要时重新取得同意。

Material updates are published here with a revised date. Affected authorized users will be notified before changes to previously authorized data uses take effect, and renewed consent will be obtained where required.

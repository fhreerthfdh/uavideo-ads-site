隐私政策
Privacy Policy

生效及最后更新：2026 年 11 月 7 日 · Effective: November 7, 2026

1. 适用范围与联系

本政策适用于 UAVideo Ads 本地广告管理工具及本公开介绍网站。工具用于运营者自有或获得明确管理授权的 Google Ads 账户。开发者及隐私联系邮箱：honormagic8@gmail.com。

This policy covers the UAVideo Ads local operations tool and its public informational website. The tool serves the operator’s own or explicitly authorized Google Ads accounts. Contact: honormagic8@gmail.com.

2. 访问和处理的数据

在账户持有人授权后，工具可处理 Google Ads 账户标识及配置、广告系列和广告组、素材与广告文案、预算及出价配置，以及展示、点击、费用、转化和转化价值等汇总表现数据。工具还可保存操作者提供的素材、操作指令、API 响应、错误信息及操作回执。

OAuth 访问凭据用于调用获授权的 API。工具不要求用户向开发者提供 Google 密码，公开网站不收集登录凭据。

Authorized data may include account identifiers and settings, campaigns, ad groups, creatives, ad copy, budgets, bidding settings, and aggregate performance metrics. Local records may include supplied assets, instructions, API responses, errors, and operation receipts. OAuth credentials enable authorized API calls; Google passwords are not requested.

3. 数据用途

数据用于展示和分析广告表现、制作本地报表、管理素材、执行操作者明确指定的广告操作，以及排查故障、避免重复操作和保留审计记录。仅请求实现这些功能所需的访问权限。

Data is used for reporting, advertising analysis, creative management, explicitly requested account operations, troubleshooting, duplicate-operation prevention, and audit records.

4. 存储、访问与保留

运行配置、凭据、报表及操作记录由运营者在其控制的本地环境中管理。报告和业务记录保留至运营者删除，或不再为上述用途所需要时；备份如存在，也需要由运营者单独清理。访问应限于获得授权的运营人员及其明确选用的处理服务。公开的 GitHub Pages 仓库不用于存储广告账户数据或密钥。

Configuration, credentials, reports, and operation records are managed in the operator-controlled local environment. Business records remain until deleted or no longer needed for the stated purposes; any backups require separate cleanup. Access is limited to authorized operators and processing services they explicitly select. The public Pages repository is not used to store advertising account data or secrets.

5. 数据分享与第三方服务

执行账户操作时，相关请求和素材会发送给 Google。操作者可能选择辅助分析、文案或素材处理服务；提供给这些服务的信息应限于完成明确任务所必需的内容，并由操作者按相应授权和服务条款管理。Google OAuth 凭据不应作为提示词或公开内容发送给这些服务。

UAVideo Ads 不出售 Google 用户数据，不将其提供给数据经纪商或用于与该工具功能无关的广告定向。通过 Google API 获得的数据不会被用于训练通用人工智能或机器学习模型。依法必须提供的信息，按适用法律处理。

UAVideo Ads 对从 Google API 收到的信息的使用及向其他应用的转移，将遵守 Google API Services User Data Policy，包括其 Limited Use 要求。

Google receives requests and assets needed for authorized operations. The operator may explicitly select analysis or creative-processing services and must limit shared information to the requested task. OAuth credentials must not be shared as prompts or public content. Google user data is not sold, provided to data brokers, used for unrelated ad targeting, or used to train general-purpose AI or machine-learning models. UAVideo Ads’ use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including Limited Use requirements.

6. 撤销授权与删除

您可以在 Google 账户的第三方连接页面撤销应用授权。撤销可阻止后续使用该授权访问 Google 数据，但不会自动删除已经保存的本地报表、备份或 Google Ads 中的广告对象。运营者可清理本地凭据及记录；其他获授权使用者可通过上述邮箱提出访问、更正或删除请求。请勿通过邮件发送密码或访问令牌。

Revoke access in your Google Account’s third-party connections settings. Revocation does not automatically erase existing local reports, backups, or Google Ads resources. Operators can remove local credentials and records. Authorized users may request access, correction, or deletion by email; do not send passwords or access tokens.

7. 网站访问与政策更新

本介绍网站不设置登录表单，不主动添加分析追踪或广告脚本。网站由 GitHub Pages 托管；GitHub 会按其 隐私声明处理访问日志等数据。政策变更会在本页更新日期并公布。

This informational site has no login form, added analytics trackers, or advertising scripts. GitHub Pages hosts the site and processes access data under GitHub’s privacy statement. Policy updates will be published here with a revised date.

Privacy Policy

Application: UAVideo Ads

Effective and last updated: November 7, 2026

1. Scope and contact

This Privacy Policy applies to the UAVideo Ads local advertising management and reporting application and its public informational website. The application is used for the operator's own Google Ads accounts or accounts they are explicitly authorized to manage. It does not offer public account registration.

The developer and privacy contact is honormagic8@gmail.com. Questions and data requests should identify UAVideo Ads and the relevant data or account, without including passwords, access tokens or private keys.

2. Information accessed and collected

After authorization, UAVideo Ads accesses the following information as needed for the requested operation:

  • Advertising account information: customer identifiers, accessible account settings and configuration relevant to the operation.
  • Advertising resources: campaigns, ad groups, ads, creative assets and copy, asset identifiers, budgets, bids and related configuration.
  • Aggregate performance information: impressions, clicks, costs, conversions, conversion values and requested reporting dimensions.
  • Operator-supplied content and local records: images, videos, ad copy, operation instructions, exported reports, API responses, errors, review records and operation receipts.
  • Authorization information: OAuth credentials or operator-configured service-account credentials used to authorize Google API requests.

The Google Ads API scope is https://www.googleapis.com/auth/adwords. This authorization does not grant the application access to Gmail messages, contacts or private Google Drive files. The tool does not request individual ad viewers' identity profiles; reports mainly process account-level and ad-level aggregate metrics. The application does not ask users to provide their Google password to the developer.

3. How the information is used

Google Ads data is used to display and analyze advertising performance, produce local reports, organize and review creative assets, execute explicitly requested advertising operations, diagnose errors, avoid duplicate operations and preserve operation records. Access is limited to the permissions needed for these functions.

Credentials are used to authenticate authorized API requests. They are not included in the public website or supplied as prompts to assistance services. Advertising data is not published through this informational site.

4. Sharing, transfers and human access

  • Google: receives authorization requests and the account operations, settings or creative content needed to perform the operator's requested Google Ads actions.
  • Authorized operators: the operator and people they explicitly authorize may inspect local reports, creatives and records for analysis, review, support and troubleshooting.
  • Explicitly selected processing services: the operator may provide selected reports, copy or assets to assistance services such as OpenAI / Codex. Such transfers must be authorized, necessary for the explicitly requested functionality, and handled under the applicable processing terms and settings. They do not permit sale of Google user data or general-purpose model training.
  • GitHub Pages: hosts only the public application information and policy pages. GitHub processes site access logs under its own privacy statement; advertising account records and credentials are not stored in this public repository.
  • Legal requirements: information may be disclosed where required by applicable law.

UAVideo Ads does not sell Google user data or provide it to data brokers or information resellers. It does not use Google user data to build unrelated advertising profiles, determine creditworthiness, or train general-purpose AI or machine-learning models. Google Workspace APIs are not used by this application to develop, improve or train non-personalized AI or machine-learning models.

UAVideo Ads' use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

5. Security and storage

Requests to Google OAuth and Google Ads API endpoints use HTTPS/TLS to protect data in transit. Credentials are configured separately in the operator-controlled local environment and are not embedded in public pages or public source code. Reports and operation receipts are saved to local directories controlled by the operator. This website has no account-data download or account-operation interface.

Local access protection relies on operating-system accounts, file permissions and device security. The application does not add encryption at rest or automatic scheduled deletion; this policy does not claim that all local files are encrypted. Operators must restrict device and backup access and must not publicly share credentials or unsanitized reports.

6. Data retention

OAuth or service-account credentials remain until replaced, revoked or deleted. Reports, exports, API responses, creatives and operation receipts are retained for the operator's reporting, operational, troubleshooting and audit needs. They should be removed when no longer needed or following a verified deletion request, unless retention is required by law.

There is no universal automatic expiry or scheduled deletion mechanism. The operator must also remove relevant exports and operator-controlled backups. Removing local records does not automatically remove advertising resources already created in Google Ads. Google controls retention of data on its own systems under its policies.

7. Access, export, correction and deletion

Operators can stop running UAVideo Ads and remove local credentials, reports, exports, API receipts, creatives and backups they control. Other authorized users can request access, correction, export or deletion by emailing honormagic8@gmail.com with the subject UAVideo Ads data request. Specify the relevant account and requested data scope without sending passwords, tokens or private keys.

The developer will verify the requester's authority over the relevant account before processing the request and will explain completion or any legally required retention. A deletion request concerns records controlled by the operator or developer; Google-held resources and data remain subject to Google's controls and policies.

8. Revoking Google access

For user OAuth, remove UAVideo Ads in your Google Account's third-party connections settings. For service accounts, an administrator can revoke the service account's Google Ads access or disable its relevant keys. Revocation prevents subsequent access under that authorization but does not automatically erase existing local files. Follow the deletion steps above to remove previously saved records.

9. Website access and policy changes

This static informational website has no login form, added analytics trackers or advertising scripts. GitHub Pages may process IP addresses and other access information for hosting and security under the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

Updates to this policy are published here with a revised date. Affected authorized users will be notified before material changes to previously authorized uses of their Google data take effect, and renewed consent will be obtained where required.
